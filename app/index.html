        How long Indonesians live, and how healthy their children are — four indicators, each sourced.

        Reading the numbers

        Life expectancy has risen steadily for a decade, to 74.15 years in 2024. Stunting — children too short for their age — fell to 19.8% of under-fives in 2024, still the health system's biggest unfinished job. Maternal mortality, 173 deaths per 100,000 live births in 2023, is the figure the government tracks against its 2030 target.

        Sources: BPS 2024; Ministry of Health (SSGI) 2024; World Bank 2023.

        Schooling in Indonesia: near-universal literacy, nine years of schooling on average, and a third of young people reaching university.

        Reading the numbers

        Adult literacy reached 96.0% in 2024, and primary school enrolment is effectively universal at 97.9%. The gap opens after that: the average Indonesian aged 25 and over has 9.0 years of schooling, and 31.4% of the tertiary-age population is enrolled in higher education — the number the government is trying hardest to lift.

        Sources: BPS 2024 and 2023.

        Where every number comes from, and what this site is.

        Sources

        • BPS — Statistics Indonesia (bps.go.id): population, GDP, growth, inflation, unemployment, poverty, literacy, schooling, enrolment, trade.
        • Ministry of Health, Indonesian Nutrition Status Survey (SSGI) 2024: stunting in children under five.
        • World Bank (data.worldbank.org), 2023: maternal mortality ratio.

        Methods

        Figures are rounded for display (population to one decimal, rates to two). Provincial density is computed from the published population and area figures. Where a figure is an estimate, the text says so.

        About this site

        Nusantara in Numbers is an original, independent presentation of official statistics. It is not affiliated with BPS or the Government of Indonesia, and it copies no other site's design, text or images. It runs entirely from its own files — no network request — on a desktop or a phone.
